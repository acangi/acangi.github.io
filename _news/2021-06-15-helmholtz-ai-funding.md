The Helmholtz Association's Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Unit (Helmholtz AI) funded the project SynRap — Machine-learning based synthetic data generation for rapid physics modeling. Together with the Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron (DESY, Hamburg), the department will investigate whether neural networks can rapidly generate synthetic data on the behaviour of physical systems. SynRap was one of fifteen teams selected competitively, sharing a total of 6.2 million euros over the coming years.
