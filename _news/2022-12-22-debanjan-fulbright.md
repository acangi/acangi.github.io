Debanjan Konar received a Fulbright-Nehru fellowship, awarded through a highly competitive selection process across scientific disciplines to support early-career Indian researchers in a long-term stay at a top US university. From March 2023 the fellowship funds two years of his research at Purdue University, where he will focus on quantum machine learning — using quantum-computing concepts to design more accurate and efficient learning algorithms.
