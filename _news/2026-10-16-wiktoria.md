Wiktoria Szopa has rejoined the department as a PhD student after completing her MSc in Physics at the Warsaw University of Technology. Her doctoral project, *Computational Modeling of THz-Driven Transport in Solid Ionic Conductors*, uses atomistic simulations to connect microscopic ion dynamics in solid ionic conductors with their measurable terahertz conductivity and dielectric response. She was previously with the group as a student research assistant on a Scultetus Early Career Fellowship, modelling memristors with atomistic simulations and machine learning for her master's thesis.
