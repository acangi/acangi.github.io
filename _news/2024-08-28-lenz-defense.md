Lenz Fiedler successfully defended his PhD at the Technische Universität Dresden, becoming the first graduate to carry out his doctoral research in the department. His thesis developed a machine-learning model intended to replace conventional density functional theory calculations and thereby accelerate HPC simulations in materials science, and during his PhD he became one of the main developers of the [Materials Learning Algorithms](https://github.com/mala-project/mala) software package.
