Helmholtz AI has selected the project MLGREEN (Machine Learning Green Functions for Magnetic Materials and Spintronics) for funding. MLGREEN aims to replace the cubic scaling of the KKR Green function method with linear scaling through machine learning. Success would speed up the search for permanent magnets that avoid rare-earth elements and for spintronic devices; CASUS at HZDR leads the project, with partners at Forschungszentrum Jülich.
