Johannes Wasmer is visiting the department as a PhD student. At Forschungszentrum Jülich he is a doctoral candidate in the Quantum Theory of Materials division of the Peter Grünberg Institute, and his stay here is supported by the HIDA Trainee Network Programme, which links Helmholtz centres and fosters an interdisciplinary data-science community. During the visit he will work on spin models and machine learning.
