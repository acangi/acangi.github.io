Tejaswini Golla joined the department on 1 October 2026 as a visiting PhD student from IIITDM Kurnool, India. A DAAD scholarship under the *Bi-nationally Supervised Doctoral Degrees* programme supports her two-year stay, during which she will screen Janus van der Waals heterostructures at high throughput for photocatalytic applications, combining density functional theory with machine learning to find promising materials for photocatalytic water splitting.
