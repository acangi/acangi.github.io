Karan Shah was selected as a fellow of the Data Science Education Community of Practice (DSECOP) programme of the American Physical Society. DSECOP develops teaching materials that embed artificial intelligence and machine-learning applications in existing undergraduate physics courses, taught in the context of physics problems rather than as a separate machine-learning course.
