The Federal Ministry for the Environment, Climate Action, Nature Conservation, and Nuclear Safety (BMUKN) is funding MALEK, a three-year project that develops new machine-learning methods for long-term safety assessments of deep geological repositories holding high-level radioactive waste. HZDR/CASUS, TU Bergakademie Freiberg and TU Darmstadt are partners in the project.
