Karan Shah was accepted into the American Physical Society's Data Science Education Community of Practice (DSECOP) programme for a second time. Led by the Topical Group on Data Science and funded by the APS, DSECOP develops teaching modules that bring data-science skills into undergraduate physics curricula and bridge the gap between physics education and the skills physicists need in industry.
