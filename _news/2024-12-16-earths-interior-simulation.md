Together with collaborators from Sandia National Laboratories and the French Alternative Energies and Atomic Energy Commission (CEA), the department has introduced a simulation method that describes not only the behaviour of atoms but also the magnetic properties of materials, promising new insight into the Earth's core. Beyond geophysics, the approach could support future technologies such as neuromorphic computing, and the work was published in [PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2408897121).
