Wiktoria Szopa joined the department as a student research assistant with support from a Scultetus Early Career Fellowship, which funds a six-month research stay for her master's thesis. She is completing a master's in applied physics at the Warsaw University of Technology and models memristors using first-principles and atomistic simulations together with machine learning; because the resistance of these nanoscale components depends on the history of the current passing through them, they are promising for neuromorphic computing. The fellowship is intended to give young researchers early scientific independence and international visibility.
