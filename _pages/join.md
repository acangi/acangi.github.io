We are a young, interdisciplinary department at the [Center for Advanced Systems Understanding
(CASUS)](https://www.casus.science), part of [Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf
(HZDR)](https://www.hzdr.de), located in **Görlitz** on the German–Polish border. We combine
quantum-mechanical theory, physics-informed machine learning, and high-performance computing to
understand and design materials — from electronic structure and chemical representation learning
to quantum dynamics, magnetism, and THz-driven matter.

We are looking for people who enjoy working at the boundary between physics, numerical methods, and
software. You do not need to arrive as an expert in everything: we value curiosity, solid
fundamentals, and the willingness to learn a new field.

## Why work here

- **Real problems, real data.** Our methods are developed together with experiments and with partner
  institutions, and are released as open-source software ([MALA]({{ '/software/' | relative_url }}),
  atoMEC) that others actually use.
- **Computing power.** You will have access to HPC systems for method development and large-scale
  production runs.
- **Interdisciplinary supervision.** Physicists, chemists, computer scientists, and research
  software engineers work in the same department, so machine-learning and domain expertise sit
  side by side.
- **International environment.** CASUS is an international institute with strong ties to
  universities and research centres in Germany, Poland, and beyond; English is the working language.
- **Quality of life.** Görlitz is a compact, affordable city with a well-preserved historic centre,
  and is within easy reach of Dresden, Wrocław, and Berlin.

## How to apply

Send your application — a single PDF containing a CV, a short statement of research interests, and
transcripts (plus contact details of two references for postdoc applications) — to
**[a.cangi@hzdr.de](mailto:a.cangi@hzdr.de)** with the subject line *[Application] <position>*.
Formal applications for advertised positions must be submitted through the HZDR job portal; the
email is for informal enquiries and for unsolicited applications.

HZDR is an equal-opportunity employer; the applicable equal-opportunity and inclusion policies are
described on the [HZDR careers pages](https://www.hzdr.de/db/Cms?pOid=11111&pNid=151).
