The Department of Machine Learning for Materials Design is a mixed team of physicists, chemists, computer scientists, and research software engineers. We work at the interface of quantum-mechanical theory, machine learning, and high-performance computing — from deriving new representations of electronic structure to shipping production-quality scientific software.

We are part of the [Center for Advanced Systems Understanding (CASUS)](https://www.casus.science) at [Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR)](https://www.hzdr.de) in Görlitz, Germany. Institutional profiles are listed on the [CASUS department page](https://www.casus.science/?page_id=4660).
